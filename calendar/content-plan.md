Thanksgiving 2026 falls on Thu 26 Nov and Black Friday on 27 Nov. Shorts for 06/07 go out daily 16–27 Nov.

Facts still to verify for 06/07:
- 1939: Thanksgiving fell on 30 Nov and retailers (Lew Hahn, Retail Dry Goods Association) asked for it to be moved. FDR moved it to 23 Nov, the states split, and Congress fixed the 4th Thursday in 1941.
- "Black Friday" was a Philadelphia police term (1950s–60s). The "red to black" story is a later retailer rebrand (~1980s).
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Friday_etymology_hoax , https://historynet.com/fdrs-franksgiving-debacle/
