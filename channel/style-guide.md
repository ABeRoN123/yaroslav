# Visual style guide

## Characters (channel canon, full text in D:\клод ютуб\_персонажи\character_bible.txt)
- Big round white head, two equal fairly large round eyes with dot pupils, a tiny one-line nose, and ONE small unbroken mouth fully inside the face.
- A real clothed body (not a stick figure) with round hands.
- Women: the same, plus 3 tiny eyelashes; 10 numbered hairstyles are in mtw_woman_hairstyles.jpg.
- Colored hair (brown, black, auburn, blonde, chestnut). Gray hair only for elders. Never white hair together with white clothes.
- Use cream/ivory outfits with colored accents instead of all-white, against a contrasting plain wall.

## Frame quality rules (check every frame)
1. Crowds look like the heroes: same heads, faces and clothes, never faceless.
2. Papers are never blank. Short text goes into the prompt in quotes; long text is overlaid in the edit.
3. Hair is colored.
4. Fingers: reject only obvious deformities.
5. Faces are complete: one mouth, two equal eyes, a nose. Prompt line that helps: "Every face has two perfectly round eyes of exactly the same size, both looking in the same direction."
6. The same character keeps the same look in every shot (use a character card as reference).
7. Clothes, props, furniture and cars are correct for the era.
8. YouTube-safe: shaving or bathroom scenes are shown only by hint.
9. Each character's emotion matches the VO line.
10. Continuity between consecutive shots.
11. Real people are drawn in the channel style and recognisable by one detail.
12. No stray logos or random text.
- Reject variants where the same character is drawn twice (model-sheet look).

## Text overlays
- Fonts: Inter Display Black for titles, Inter ExtraBold for small labels. Ink color (43,50,56), amber accent (201,125,40).
- Chapter card: a "CHAPTER N" label (white or amber) above a 1–3 line title.
- Shorts layout:
  - 1080×1920 dark background (31,38,43);
  - a 2-line hook title (white line + yellow line);
  - the 16:9 clip at y=560;
