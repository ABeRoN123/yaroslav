# Positioning

**Made to Want: who decided what you want?**

Short, hand-drawn doodle video essays that uncover the real marketing history behind everyday "normal" things: wedding rings, mouthwash, breakfast, shaving, pink for girls. Each episode takes a habit everyone thinks is natural or ancient and shows the exact ad campaign, company and year that invented it.

- Format: long videos of 5–10 min with English VO, plus 5–6 Shorts per video that are 25–35 s each with a fact-first hook.
- Tone: curious and slightly ironic, never preachy. The ending is always "do what you like, just know whose idea it was", followed by the line "Made to Want."
- Structure:
  - cold-open hook that states the twist in the first sentence;
  - title card;
  - chapters;
  - "why it still works" (the recurring playbook: give a normal thing an ugly name → tie the fix to being modern → hide the product → make it repeat forever);
  - callback ending.
- Content pillars: invented traditions (diamond ring, gender colors), invented problems (halitosis, body hair), invented "health" (breakfast, cereal), invented holidays and shopping days (Thanksgiving date, Black Friday).
